Standard Terms & Conditions
A. Listing Exclusivity and Duration
Duration: All Exclusive Right to Sell agreements are signed for a period of six (6) months.

Protection Clause: If the property sells within 90 days after the agreement expires to a party introduced by Apex Horizon during the contract term, the agreed commission is still due.

B. Payment and Closing
Commissions are paid in full only upon the successful closing and transfer of title. All funds are deducted by the closing attorney/title company and disbursed accordingly.

NYC Co-op Fee: Seller is responsible for any applicable co-op board package processing fees (typically non-refundable).

C. Termination Policy
Client Termination (No Fault): If the Client terminates the contract before the term expires, a marketing reimbursement fee of $750 will be due upon cancellation to cover non-recoverable advertising costs.

Performance Review: If no offers are received within 120 days at the mutually agreed-upon list price, the contract may be terminated by the client without penalty.
